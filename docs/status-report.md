## Environment limitations encountered

- `release.anza.xyz`, `github.com` release downloads and Solana RPC hosts were denied by the sandbox egress policy. The Solana/Anchor toolchain was obtained from Docker Hub (`solanafoundation/anchor:v1.0.2`) and crates.io/npm were reachable, so compilation, SBF build and a local validator were possible. Devnet was not.
- The Agave 3.x test validator requires io_uring; Docker's default seccomp profile blocks it, so `scripts/localnet.sh` runs the container with `--security-opt seccomp=unconfined`.
- Anchor 1.x: `CpiContext` takes a program `Pubkey`; large accounts must be `Box`ed to stay within the SBF stack frame (the first unboxed build failed with a stack access violation, fixed).

## Economic findings that affect launch (summary; details in `docs/economic-findings.md`)

- At pilot defaults a fresh market absorbs at most ~1.49 SOL per buy within 32 bins, and a 1 SOL buy moves the price ~25%. Larger orders must be split.
- Graduation needs roughly 6,700 SOL of matched two-sided volume (~0.013 SOL paired per 1 SOL round trip); one actor can manufacture it for ~309 SOL in fees.
- Loose slippage tolerance (25%) made a sandwich profitable (+0.059 SOL on a 0.5 SOL victim); 1% tolerance reverted the victim's swap.
