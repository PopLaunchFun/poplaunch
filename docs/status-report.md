## Owner decisions applied on top of the v2 brief

- Buybacks kept as escrow-only (brief said remove): earmark + vault + bounded withdrawal on-chain, buy-and-burn off-program via Jupiter in the same transaction.
- Minimum seed 1 SOL per coin, creator-chosen above that.
- Coin images/descriptions are https URLs stored off-chain under a creator-signed message.
- POP mint not yet known: `set_pop_mint` publishes it later; withdrawals are blocked until then.

## Environment limitations encountered

- Solana RPC hosts, release downloads and Jupiter are denied by the sandbox egress policy; the toolchain came from Docker Hub (`solanafoundation/anchor:v1.0.2`), crates.io and npm.
- The Agave 3.x test validator needs io_uring (`--security-opt seccomp=unconfined`) and prunes its ledger quickly unless `--limit-ledger-size` is raised; both are handled by `scripts/localnet.sh`.
- Anchor's TypeScript coder under-reports the size of accounts with `#[max_len]` strings; the SDK pins the real Market size (1,224 bytes) and the suite asserts it.
